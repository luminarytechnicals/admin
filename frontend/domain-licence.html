Domain Licence & Provider Disclosure

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Purpose of this notice

This page explains the domain arrangement used by Luminary Technicals and distinguishes the website publisher from the domain namespace service and the website's hosting/deployment. It is a disclosure published by Luminary Technicals—not a licence, certificate, endorsement, or official statement issued by DigitalPlat Domains or DPDNS.

The public website address covered by this notice is luminarytechnicals.dpdns.org.

Independence clarification: Luminary Technicals is not a part of DigitalPlat Domains or DPDNS. It uses the dpdns.org domain namespace for its web address only. This does not establish common ownership, management, employment, partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement.

1. Domain name and registration context

Luminary Technicals uses luminarytechnicals.dpdns.org as its public website address. The dpdns.org namespace is listed by DigitalPlat Domains among its public namespaces, and the provider advertises free registration availability. This site's domain was obtained using the free-registration option, as applicable to this domain.

A domain name is an internet address. Its registration or delegation does not, by itself, establish who owns or operates the website hosted at that address, nor does it establish a commercial partnership with the namespace provider.

For current registration status, account-level details, renewal conditions, and the assigned nameservers, the relevant provider dashboard and registration records take precedence over this general disclosure.

3. Domain, DNS, hosting, and website publishing are separate

  • Website publisher: Luminary Technicals publishes the website content and identifies its official contact channels.
  • Domain namespace/registration service: DigitalPlat Domains provides registration workflows for public namespaces, including *.dpdns.org, subject to its policies and availability.
  • DNS configuration: DNS records and nameserver delegation determine where the domain resolves. These settings may be managed through the registration service or configured with another compatible DNS provider; check the active domain account for the current arrangement.
  • Website source and deployment: This website's source is maintained in the Luminary Technicals admin repository ↗, with deployment configured separately through Netlify.

These are distinct technical roles. Use of a domain namespace does not imply that the namespace provider wrote, reviews, hosts, certifies, sponsors, or endorses the site's content.
