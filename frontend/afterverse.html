What is Luminary Afterverse?

Luminary Afterverse is the forward-looking creative exploration initiative developed under Luminary Developers. It merges high-performance web graphics, spatial user interfaces, and generative digital atmospheres to build immersive online spaces for research, interactive storytelling, and community experiences.
