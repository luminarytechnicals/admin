AgroScan

Intelligent agricultural diagnostics combining edge computer vision with IoT micro-telemetry to identify crop diseases, preserve yields, and empower farmers with localized treatment intelligence.

Revival Program 2027 Explore Technology Project Environments
Parent Organ: Luminary Developers (CREATE) Research Track: Agricultural AI & Edge Inference Status: Active Engineering Cycle (Milestone 2027)
⚠️
The Problem

Delayed Detection & Resource Depletion

Crop diseases and soil nutrient imbalances account for up to 40% of global agricultural yield loss. In rural farming regions, access to professional plant pathologists is virtually non-existent, leading to indiscriminate chemical pesticide over-application, contaminated groundwater, and preventable crop devastation.

💡
The AgroScan Solution

Instant, Edge-Based Diagnosis & Guidance

AgroScan turns ordinary smartphones and low-cost optical field kits into instant plant pathologists. Utilizing lightweight quantized neural networks running offline in the browser, AgroScan identifies foliar pathogens from a simple photograph in under 150ms and provides actionable, organic or targeted remediation plans.

Core Technology Stack

Combining state-of-the-art vision models, hardware telemetry, and resilient software.

🤖

AI & COMPUTER VISION

Mobile-optimized Vision Transformers (ViT) and pruned MobileNet architectures trained on over 120,000 foliar pathology images. Capable of classifying 42 common crop diseases with >94% top-1 accuracy.

📡

EDGE HARDWARE & SENSORS

ESP32 microcontrollers paired with multispectral optical lenses, NPK soil probes, and LoRa wireless transmitters for ambient temperature, soil humidity, and pH telemetry spanning 5km mesh radius.

⚡

OFFLINE-FIRST PWA

Constructed with pure semantic HTML5, Web Workers, and IndexedDB model weight caching. Allows zero-connectivity rural diagnostics with deferred cloud synchronization once cellular networks are re-established.

AgroScan Revival Program 2027

A multi-year roadmap returning AgroScan to production field validation and farmer enablement.

PHASE 1 · Q3-Q4 2025

Model Quantization & Dataset Expansion

Consolidating over 120,000 multi-regional pathology samples and quantizing transformer layers down to 8.4MB for browser client memory safety.

PHASE 2 · Q1-Q2 2026

Sensor Telemetry Firmware & LoRa Mesh

Engineering low-power ESP32 field units capable of 12-month battery life on solar trickle charge, transmitting soil conditions to village gateway hubs.

PHASE 3 · Q3-Q4 2026

Closed Agronomic Field Trials

Deploying 50 pilot nodes across agricultural testing plots in India to benchmark diagnostic precision against certified agronomist lab assessments.

PHASE 4 · Q1 2027

Public Launch & Open Agronomy API

Global availability of the AgroScan PWA, multi-language voice diagnostic interface for rural farmers, and open agronomy research API under Luminary Developers.
