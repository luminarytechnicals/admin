Shareholding Review & IPO Notice — 12 March 2025

A dated management-supplied shareholding statement and pause notice. Ownership percentages, historical growth and exchange-trading claims require supporting documentation.

By Luminary Technicals✦12 Mar 2025✦Management-supplied notice · Verification required

This page records a management-supplied statement dated 12 March 2025 about a stated shareholding distribution and a pause on new IPO or issuance activity until a further declaration. It is not an independent audit, a prospectus, or proof of stock-exchange listing.

Verification status: The ownership percentages, the claim of more than 172,000% growth, the names in the supplied schedule, and the assertion that existing shares can be traded on NSE and BSE have not been independently verified for this publication. This page does not establish corporate ownership, third-party endorsement, listing status, or investment performance.

1. Notice and scope

This notice records a management-supplied position dated 12 March 2025: no new IPO or new issuance for holders will be announced until a subsequent formal declaration. The information supplied does not provide a detailed or formally documented reason for the pause, so this article does not invent one.

The supplied note frames the pause as a period for economic consolidation and governance review. That is a high-level description, not a substantiated financial finding. This notice does not confirm that an IPO had previously been formally approved, filed, or accepted by an exchange or regulator.

The following allocation is reproduced from the information supplied for publication. It has not been independently audited or matched to a formal share register or allotment records.

3. Reported growth since March 2023

The supplied statement describes growth of more than 172,000% beginning in March 2023 and spanning roughly the following two years. The material provided does not clarify what the figure measures, the starting and ending values, the calculation method, the valuation basis, or whether an independent auditor reviewed the result.

For transparency, this is presented only as a claim in the supplied statement. It must not be read as a verified share-price return, independently established valuation increase, promise of future performance, or guarantee of investor gains. Supporting calculations and dated primary records are needed before it can be reported as verified performance.

4. Pause on new IPO or issuance activity

The notice states that no new IPO or new issuance will be announced for holders until a further formal declaration. No date for that next declaration was supplied. The notice should be read as the stated management position, not as evidence of regulatory approval or an exchange-accepted filing.

This webpage does not amend a shareholder's legal rights, change any share register, authorize a transfer, or establish whether any security is publicly listed. Those matters depend on formal issuer records and applicable requirements.

5. NSE and BSE trading status must be verified

The information supplied for the requested notice states that existing shares may be bought and sold on both the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) and BSE. However, no official issuer listing name, ticker or scrip code, ISIN, exchange circular, or other primary listing record was supplied, and listing status could not be independently verified for this page.

Accordingly, this article does not confirm that Luminary Technicals shares are listed or tradable on NSE or BSE. Before considering any transaction, verify the exact legal issuer and security directly against official exchange records and consult a registered broker or depository participant. Do not rely on this webpage alone as evidence that a security exists, is listed, or can be traded.

6. Records needed to substantiate the statement

A meaningful shareholding audit or public disclosure should be supported by the legal name and registration details of the issuer, a dated share register, allotment and transfer records, relevant board or shareholder resolutions, and an independent auditor's report where applicable. A claimed exchange listing should be matched to the official security identifier, exchange symbol or scrip code, ISIN, and relevant exchange record.

The growth figure should identify what was measured and provide dated starting and ending values, the calculation method, valuation or market-price basis, and records supporting the result. Without those documents, the figures remain supplied claims rather than independently verified findings.

7. Next declaration and governance review

The next formal declaration should, where authorized for disclosure, identify the decision-maker, clarify the pause's scope and duration, explain the reason, and state whether it concerns a proposed IPO, a new primary issuance, or another specific action. Any future update should be dated and linked to relevant supporting documents.

Until such a declaration is published, this page records only the information supplied for 12 March 2025. It does not predict when an offering will occur or make a promise about the value or liquidity of any holding.

8. Important limitation

This is an informational website article, not an investment offer, solicitation, recommendation, or independent audit opinion. The percentages, names, growth claim, and exchange-trading statement are not independently verified by this publication. This article does not establish the legal identity or status of an issuer, confirm third-party ownership, or prove an NSE or BSE listing.
