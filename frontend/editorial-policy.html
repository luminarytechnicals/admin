Editorial Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026 • Published by Luminary Technicals

Purpose & Scope

Luminary Technicals produces technical research, documentation, architectural blueprints, educational guides, and ecosystem news. This Editorial Policy governs all content published across our primary domain, subsidiary platforms, and research initiatives.

1. Factual Accuracy & Technical Verification

Every technical paper, documentation release, and article undergoes rigorous internal review. We verify code snippets, architectural specifications, and empirical claims against working prototypes and reproducible benchmarks.

2. Transparency of Stages & Distinctions

To prevent misleading claims, Luminary Technicals explicitly categorizes initiatives into distinct lifecycle stages:

  • Concept: Theoretical frameworks or exploratory proposals.
  • Research: Active experimentation, simulations, and data modeling.
  • Prototype / Lab: Working alpha/beta software or test deployments (e.g., Afterverse Lab).
  • Operational: Production-ready systems, live services, and deployed infrastructure.

3. Authorship & Attribution

Articles, whitepapers, and technical guides clearly credit primary authors and contributors (such as Founder & Digital Architect AR. Abhinav Ranjan and affiliated researchers). External references, open-source libraries, and foundational literature are cited accurately.

4. Corrections & Updates

When factual errors or outdated technical specs are identified, we promptly issue transparent corrections and update timestamps on the affected documentation. Inquiries or correction requests can be submitted directly to luminarytechnicals@gmail.com.
