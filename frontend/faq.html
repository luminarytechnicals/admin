Luminary Technicals is a multidisciplinary technology and innovation ecosystem connecting digital infrastructure, software development, research, cybersecurity, education, collaboration, and public-interest initiatives.
The name represents a technology-focused ecosystem intended to build, research, secure, empower, and connect people, projects, systems, and ideas through technology.
Its purpose is to bring technology development, digital infrastructure, research, education, cybersecurity, creative experimentation, and public-interest work into one connected ecosystem.
The vision is to create a connected technology ecosystem where infrastructure, developers, researchers, educators, creators, institutions, and communities can build and share useful digital systems.
The mission is to build practical technology, conduct research, improve digital security, support learning, create useful services, and connect people and institutions through collaborative projects.
Luminary Technicals emphasizes creation, research, responsible technology, security, education, collaboration, experimentation, community service, and practical public benefit.
The organizational formula is POWER. CREATE. EMPOWER. CONNECT. ENGAGE., representing Servers, Developers, Cares, Kits, and Federals respectively.
The public-facing formula is BUILD. RESEARCH. SECURE. EMPOWER. CONNECT., summarizing the broader public identity and purpose of the ecosystem.
